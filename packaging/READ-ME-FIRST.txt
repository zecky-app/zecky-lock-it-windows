Zecky Lock it for Windows - 0.1.0 beta 1

A calm, self-custody Zcash wallet. Your 24 words stay on this PC, sealed by
the keys you choose (Windows Hello, a passphrase, a Zecky code, a security
key). Go Max runs your own pruned Zcash node. Seal it lets a phone sign.

THIS IS A BETA. Use small amounts only (under 0.1 ZEC).

Not code-signed: Windows SmartScreen will warn. Click "More info", then
"Run anyway". Check the download first: see VERIFY.txt.

Keep this folder together:
  Zecky Lock it.exe      the app
  WebView2Loader.dll     must stay next to the app
  params\                the Sapling files a payment needs (from Zcash)
  node\zakurad.exe       Zakura 1.8.5 (Go Max's node)
  node\zainod.exe        Zaino 0.10.1 (Go Max's wallet service)

Needs: Windows 10 (1903 or later) or 11, x64, internet, Microsoft Edge
WebView2 (already on almost every Windows 10/11 PC). Go Max needs about
35 GB free on drive C:. Seal it needs a webcam.

Where your wallet is kept: %LOCALAPPDATA%\app.zecky.lockit
  wallet-lock.json   your 24 words, sealed by your keys
  keyring.json       extra wallets' words, sealed with the main words
  wallet\            the wallet's own files (no words in them)
  go-max\            Go Max's node data
Removing the app does not remove this folder.

Your 24 words are shown once, when you create a wallet. Write them on
paper. They are the only way back if this PC is lost.

Seal it (signing on your phone) is experimental on Windows: try it with a
tiny amount first.

IF SOMETHING DOESN'T WORK

"Windows protected your PC"
  Lock it is not code-signed (that costs money every year). Click "More
  info", then "Run anyway". Check your download first: see VERIFY.txt.

Your antivirus removes or blocks a file
  New, unsigned programs are sometimes flagged, and Go Max's node programs
  (node\zakurad.exe, node\zainod.exe) most of all. Check the download with
  VERIFY.txt, then allow the file in your antivirus and install again. The
  wallet itself works without Go Max.

Sending fails over Tor
  Some networks (offices, schools, public Wi-Fi) block Tor. Press "Send
  without Tor", or try again on another network. It is the same payment
  each time, so nothing is paid twice.

Restoring takes long
  Lock it checks every block from when your words were made. With the exact
  birthday block it starts right there; with only a month, a few hours for
  an old wallet is normal. The progress shows on the home screen.

Windows Hello is greyed out
  Set up a PIN, face or fingerprint in Windows Settings > Accounts > Sign-in
  options first. Or use a passphrase as your key.

"Can't reach" the server
  Check the PC's internet connection and clock. Or pick another server in
  Settings > Network.

Go Max stopped while starting
  Press Try again. Still not working? Quit Lock it, open it again; if it
  still stops, Remove node and its data and switch Go Max on again.

Found a problem? In the app: Settings > About > Copy a problem report, then
open an issue on the GitHub page you downloaded this from:
  https://github.com/zecky-app/zecky-lock-it-windows/issues
The report has no words, keys, addresses or amounts.
