        Erase everything

        Removes the wallet, its words, the history and settings from this PC. Go Max's node data stays. Make sure the 24 words are on paper first.

        Ask when opening Lock it

        Windows Hello when Lock it starts, and after it locked itself (sleep, 5 minutes away). Why: your balance and history stay private on a shared or unattended PC.

        Zecky code

        Six digits only you know, asked with your other keys every time the lock opens. Why: someone who knows your Windows PIN still cannot take anything out.

        Security key

        A hardware key, such as a YubiKey, plugged into this PC or held to it, touched every time the lock opens. Why: Windows Hello, your passphrase and the Zecky code all live on this PC; a key you carry is something a thief of the PC does not have.

        Removing all security keys

        Lost your keys?

        Remove all security keys after 48 hours. Slow on purpose: someone at your PC cannot simply take the keys away, and you have time to notice and cancel.

        Really open the lock?

        A pause before your keys are asked, with the reminder that nobody legitimate rushes you.

        Always asked, whatever you choose: showing the recovery phrase, the viewing key and erasing. Making a key stricter needs nothing; weakening one asks your keys first. The keys themselves are chosen when the wallet is made. The keys and the time lock apply to every wallet on this PC.

        Time lock

        Opening the lock has to be asked for this long ahead. Why: for Zcash you mean to keep, a wait of days protects you against pressure and impulse. Turning it off or shorter waits as long as the current time lock. In an emergency you can break the glass. It does not stop someone who has your 24 words. It applies to every wallet on this PC.

        Recovery phrase

        The 24 words of this wallet, sealed by the keys you chose. Why: the 24 words are the wallet. Sealed, they cannot be read from a copy of this PC's files.

          Hidden again after 2 minutes, or when Lock it is not in front.

          Viewing key

          A viewing key gives read-only access to this wallet. Whoever has it can see your balance and every payment in and out, past and future, but cannot spend anything.

          It cannot be taken back once shared. Give it only to someone or something that should see everything, such as your own watch-only device or an accountant.

          Saved addresses

          Saved addresses you trust. Pick a name when sending instead of pasting an address, so a swapped address stands out. Kept inside the lock: every change asks your keys, so nobody can quietly add an address of their own.
